phiPICT Documentation

phiPICT is a differentiable, GPU-accelerated multi-block PISO solver for PyTorch, based on PICT. On top of the incompressible Navier-Stokes solver it adds inductionless magnetohydrodynamics (MHD) with insulating and thin conducting walls, relative solver tolerances and an AMG-preconditioned potential solve.
